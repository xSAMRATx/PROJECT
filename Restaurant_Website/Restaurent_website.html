About Us

Spicy King started as a small family kitchen with one simple idea: serve fresh, full-flavoured food at a fair price. Today we cook every dish to order using fresh ingredients delivered every morning, from crispy chicken rolls and juicy burgers to slow-cooked beef and colourful vegetable plates. Our chefs mix traditional spices with modern recipes, so there is something for everyone, whether you like it mild or extra hot. Come in for a relaxed dinner with friends and family, grab a quick takeaway on your way home, or order online for delivery. We are open six days a week and every visit comes with a warm welcome, friendly service and portions that leave you happy. Thank you for choosing Spicy King, where every meal is made with love and a little bit of spice.
